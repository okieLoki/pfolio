date: 2026-07-15
excerpt: A short list of software and hardware I actually rely on. No affiliate links, no sponsored picks.
---

People ask, so here's the list. It's short on purpose. If something isn't here, it's because I tried it and stopped.

## Hardware

- A laptop with a good keyboard and a screen I don't think about.
- One external monitor. Two made me worse at focusing.
- Cheap wired earphones. They never need charging.

## Software

- **A terminal.** Most of my day happens here.
- **A plain text editor** with as few plugins as I can get away with.
- **A notes folder** of Markdown files, synced by whatever happens to be on the machine.
- **A browser** with an ad blocker and no extensions beyond that.

## Not on the list

Task managers, habit trackers, dashboards, second brains. I've owned all of them. They mostly produced a feeling of productivity rather than the thing itself.

The pattern, if there is one: every tool here is boring, has been around a while, and would be easy to replace. That turns out to be exactly what I want from a tool.
