---
title: Notes from a week without social media
date: 2026-08-22
excerpt: I deleted all apps for seven days. Here's what I noticed about my attention, my boredom, and how I spend time.
---

I deleted all apps for seven days. Not a detox, not a challenge — I just wanted to see what my hands would do when they reached for my phone and found nothing there.

## Day one and two

Reflex. I picked up the phone maybe forty times the first day, unlocked it, and stared at the home screen. It was embarrassing how physical the habit was. By the second day the reaching slowed down, but the itch didn't.

## The boredom

By mid-week something shifted. Waiting for a coffee, I just… waited. Looked around. Boredom came back, and with it, a kind of low hum of ideas. Nothing profound. But I noticed I was thinking in longer sentences again.

## What I actually missed

Less than I expected. Two group chats. One person's photos. That's it. Everything else was ambient noise I had mistaken for staying informed.

## After

I reinstalled one app, with no notifications, and it lives on the last page. The rest I haven't missed. The main thing I learned is that my attention isn't something I lose — it's something I hand over, forty times a day, without noticing.
